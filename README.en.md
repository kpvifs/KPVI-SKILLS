# KPVI-SKILLS

[简体中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)

Frameworks for three Claude Code Skills.

| Directory | Skill | Scope | Based on |
|---|---|---|---|
| `shuofu-jiaolian/` | Persuasion Coach (说服教练) | What to say: whether persuasion is the right tool, whether the audience is opposed, neutral or supportive, which point to move them to, and which method to use. | The 35 lessons of Huang Chih-Chung's (黄执中) course "35-Day Persuasion Bootcamp" (说服力三十五日训练营) |
| `biaoda-jiaolian/` | Expression Coach (表达教练) | How to say it: structure, transitions, examples, rhetorical questions, recaps. | Same as above |
| `yunying-guwen/` | Operations Advisor (运营顾问) | Operating a shop, account, product or service: traffic and algorithms, lead generation, conversion, average order value, product selection and business models, personal IP, content, private traffic, communities. | The 18 lessons of the course "7分课·运营" |

Persuasion Coach and Expression Coach support four modes: reviewing a draft or script, proposing an approach for a scenario, role-play practice, and explaining a concept or technique.

Operations Advisor supports four modes: diagnosing an operations problem, proposing a plan, explaining a concept, and reviewing an existing plan.

## What this repository contains

- Only the three `SKILL.md` files: role, decision flow, modes and output rules.
- It does not contain the `references/` knowledge base (lesson notes, course map and summary files). The `references/` files mentioned in `SKILL.md` are not in this repository.
- Without the knowledge base, these Skills cannot answer from the course content.
- `SKILL.md` and the course material are written in Simplified Chinese.
